different art forms of india

india is a land of rich culture and heritage. various states of india are famous for their unique art forms.these art forms reflect the traditions,customs,and creativity of the people.

major art forms of india

  1. madhubani painting(bihar)
  2. warli painting(maharastra)
  3. pattachitra painting (odisa)
  4. tanjore painting(tamilnadu)
  5. kalamkari art (andhrapardesh)
  6. phad painting(rajasthan)
  7. gond art(madhayapardesh)
  8. miniature painting(rajasthan)

madhubani painting

madhubani painting is a traditional folk art of bihar. it is known for its bright colors and geomatric patterns.

warli painting

warli painting is a trible art form of maharastra. it uses simple shapes like circles,traingles,and squares to depict daily life.

pattachitra painting

pattachitra is a traditional painting style form odisha.it is famous for detailed artwork based on mythological stories

tanjore painting

tanjore painting originated in tamil nadu.it is known for rich colors,gold foil work,and religious themes

indian art forms preserve the cultural heritage of the country of its people.
